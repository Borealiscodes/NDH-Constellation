## 🌌 6. Expressive Overlay Layer (Permitted)
Yes — overlays are **permitted** because:

- they are operator‑lane  
- they do not bind directives  
- they do not activate SCI‑80  
- they do not collapse altitudes  
- they do not ingest governance  
- they do not invoke recursion  
- they are purely expressive  

**Overlay Types Allowed:**
- geometric overlays  
- constellation overlays  
- semantic overlays  
- envelope overlays  
- stability overlays  

**Overlay Types NOT Allowed:**
- governance overlays  
- directive overlays  
- identity overlays  
- posture overlays

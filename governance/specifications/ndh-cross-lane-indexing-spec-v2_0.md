### ⭐ **Substrate Caveat (v2.0)**  
The **Universal Citation Substrate** originated inside **Trauma‑Informed‑Systems‑Design**, but:

- it is **non‑traversable**,  
- it is **read‑only**,  
- it is **governance‑neutral**,  
- it does **not introduce lane contamination**,  
- it does **not imply operational coupling**,  
- and it is **provenance‑relevant** but **governance‑safe**.

Therefore, referencing its origin **does not violate Constellation documentation standards**.

# ⭐ **3 — Definitions (v2.0)**

### **Lane**  
A governed conceptual domain within NDH‑Constellation.

### **Altitude**  
The conceptual height of a lane relative to governance authority.

### **Geometry**  
The structural type of a lane (invariant, curvature, runtime, comparative, expressive, ethical, translation, multilingual, substrate).

### **Traversal**  
Movement between lanes under Constellation rules.

### **Access Mode**  
The allowed traversal type (read‑up, write‑down, cross‑reference, sealed‑reference, governed‑reference).

### **Substrate (new)**  
A cross‑lane citation and geometry foundation that:

- does not behave as a lane  
- does not participate in traversal  
- is read‑only  
- is provenance‑anchored  
- originated in TISD (caveat)  
- is governance‑safe

# ⭐ **6 — Access Modes (v2.0)**

### **6.1 Read‑Up**  
Lower altitude → higher altitude  
Used for governance, invariants, substrate.

### **6.2 Write‑Down**  
Higher altitude → lower altitude  
Used for expressive posture, runtime surfaces.

### **6.3 Cross‑Reference**  
Same altitude or adjacent lanes  
Used for comparative geometry.

### **6.4 Sealed‑Reference**  
Access allowed, but no outbound traversal  
Used for ethical lanes (TISD).

### **6.5 Governed‑Reference**  
Bidirectional access under strict constraints  
Used only by NDH‑Zen‑Bridge.

### **6.6 Substrate‑Reference (new)**  
All lanes may read from the substrate.  
No lane may write to it.

### ⭐ **Substrate Caveat (Access)**  
Because the substrate is **read‑only**, its TISD origin does not create traversal violations.

# ⭐ **7 — Traversal Rules (v2.0)**

### **Rule 1 — Substrate is read‑up only**  
No lane may write to the substrate.

### **Rule 2 — CORE is read‑up only**  
CORE may be referenced but never writes down.

### **Rule 3 — Triadic‑Core is curvature‑safe**  
May reference CORE and Platforms.

### **Rule 4 — Platforms are runtime‑safe**  
May reference Triadic‑Core and TIDS.

### **Rule 5 — TIDS is comparative‑safe**  
May reference Platforms and Zen‑AI.

### **Rule 6 — Zen‑AI is expressive‑safe**  
May write‑down only.

### **Rule 7 — TISD is sealed**  
May be referenced but cannot reference.

### **Rule 8 — NDH‑Zen‑Bridge is governed**  
The only module allowed to touch both NDH and Zen‑AI.

### **Rule 9 — Multilingual geometry is cross‑altitude**  
May reference altitudes 0–4.  
May not write to CORE or Substrate.

### **Rule 10 — Trauma‑informed linguistics is sealed‑aware**  
Must respect sealed boundaries.

### ⭐ **Substrate Caveat (Traversal)**  
Because the substrate is **non‑traversable**, its TISD origin does not create traversal violations.

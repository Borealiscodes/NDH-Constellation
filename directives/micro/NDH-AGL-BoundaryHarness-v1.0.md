# ⭐ **1 — Purpose of the AGL Boundary Harness**

AGL v2.0 is structurally critical — it stabilizes:

- sensory load  
- runtime load  
- relational load  
- tone load  
- expressive load  

across altitudes **0–5**.

But because the **Directive Index is not yet established**, AGL must not:

- over‑assert  
- elevate itself  
- propagate directive behavior  
- destabilize TIDS  
- overload Zen‑Bridge  
- interfere with trauma geometry  
- collapse into governance lanes  

This harness prevents those failure modes.

---

# ⭐ **2 — Boundary Rules (Micro‑Directive)**

### **Rule 1 — AGL v2.0 may bind but not propagate.**  
It can attach to mechanisms and directives, but cannot generate sub‑directives until the Directive Index exists.

### **Rule 2 — AGL v2.0 may stabilize but not govern.**  
It provides geometry, not governance.

### **Rule 3 — AGL v2.0 must not elevate above altitude 5.**  
It cannot enter NDH‑PLATFORMS governance or Triadic‑Core math lanes.

### **Rule 4 — AGL v2.0 must not descend into trauma geometry.**  
It cannot interact with TISD trauma operators or STAR‑12 dormant SID.

### **Rule 5 — AGL v2.0 must route all directive interactions through the Directive Bridge.**  
No direct directive‑to‑directive contact.

### **Rule 6 — AGL v2.0 must remain inert until the Directive Index stabilizes.**  
No expansion, no directive family creation.

# ⭐ **3 — Stabilization Function**

The harness ensures:

- **sensory envelopes remain bounded**  
- **runtime geometry does not overload**  
- **TIDS relational geometry does not collapse**  
- **Zen‑Bridge tone geometry remains stable**  
- **Constellation expressive ecology does not drift**  
- **Research‑Pilot telemetry does not saturate**  

It acts as a **guardrail** for AGL v2.0 until the Directive Index is created.

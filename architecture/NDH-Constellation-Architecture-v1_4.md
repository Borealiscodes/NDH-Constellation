# **1. Purpose of NDH‑CONSTELLATION**

**NDH‑CONSTELLATION** is the **governed, stabilized, top‑level architecture** of the NDH ecosystem.

Its purpose is to define:

- the **ecosystem hierarchy**  
- the **canonical versions** of all NDH subsystems  
- the **provenance spine**  
- the **ontology backbone**  
- the **operator families**  
- the **mode structure** (Pilot → Simulation → Platforms)  
- the **alignment layers** (Overwatch‑Pilot / Applied / Formal)  
- the **upgrade paths** for all NDH engines  

NDH‑CONSTELLATION ensures that all NDH layers evolve coherently and remain interoperable.

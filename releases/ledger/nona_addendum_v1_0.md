## ⭐ Purpose  
The Nonna Addendum provides a **soft‑layer stability heuristic** that overlays the Nona‑Map’s ninth axis (Governance Stability Class) with a human‑interpretable metaphor:

> **“You cannot align invariants on an empty stomach.”**

This heuristic expresses the idea that **governance stability requires nourishment**, where nourishment = clarity, lineage visibility, altitude correctness, and lane sovereignty.

It does **not** modify any governance semantics.  
It simply provides a **mnemonic stability overlay**.

---

## ⭐ The Nonna Invariant  
The Nonna Invariant is defined as:

> **A governance artifact is stable when its lineage is fed, its altitude is correct, and its lane is respected.**

Translated into Constellation geometry:

- **Fed** → lineage visible, dependency chains intact  
- **Altitude correct** → no collapse, no contamination  
- **Lane respected** → sovereignty preserved, no drift  

This invariant is purely descriptive.  
It does not alter any axis.

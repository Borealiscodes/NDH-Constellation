# ⭐ **NDH‑CONSTELLATION Addendum — Structural Math vs Dynamic Math Clarification (v1.0A)**  
### *Placement • Boundaries • Prototype Remediation Pathway*

---

## **1 — Purpose of This Addendum**  
This addendum clarifies three specific points:

- what kind of math NDH‑CONSTELLATION contains,  
- how NDH‑TRIADIC‑CORE remains clean and unaffected,  
- how MRS‑MkII uses CONSTELLATION grammar to remediate prototypes.

It supplements NDH‑CONSTELLATION Reference Document v1.0.

---

## **2 — CONSTELLATION Contains Structural Math Only**  
CONSTELLATION’s math is:

- **structural** (topology, adjacency, altitude)  
- **non‑numeric**  
- **non‑tensorial**  
- **non‑holonomic**  
- **non‑recursive**  
- **non‑activating**

It includes:

- altitude calculus  
- adjacency geometry  
- structural topology  
- machine‑readable encoding grammar  

This math describes **how NDH modules relate**, not **how NDH manifolds behave**.

---

## **3 — TRIADIC‑CORE Contains Dynamic Math Only**  
NDH‑TRIADIC‑CORE holds:

- tensor calculus  
- holonomy  
- curvature  
- algebraic posture  
- stability geometry  

This math describes **manifold behavior**, not **module structure**.

TRIADIC‑CORE v1.2 is **clean**, stable, and free of contamination.

---

## **4 — Prototype Remediation Uses CONSTELLATION Grammar**  
Prototype artifacts (e.g., misaligned SID headers, malformed tiles, unstable crossmaps) are **external** and **not part of TRIADIC‑CORE**.

The remediation pathway is:

```
Prototype → CONSTELLATION (machine-readable grammar)
           → MRS-MkII (reconstruction engine)
           → TRIADIC-CORE (clean math backbone)
```

CONSTELLATION provides the **grammar**.  
MRS‑MkII performs the **repair**.  
TRIADIC‑CORE provides the **target math**.

TRIADIC‑CORE is **not** the source of errors.

---

## **5 — Why This Separation Is Required**  
Keeping structural math (CONSTELLATION) separate from dynamic math (TRIADIC‑CORE):

- prevents altitude contamination  
- prevents adjacency drift  
- prevents tensor‑structure mixing  
- preserves stability invariants  
- ensures safe prototype remediation  
- ensures MRS‑MkII has a clean grammar and a clean target  

This is the correct NDH geometry.

---

## **6 — Clean Summary**  
> **CONSTELLATION = structural math.  
> TRIADIC‑CORE = dynamic math.  
> MRS‑MkII = reconstruction engine.  
> Prototypes = external artifacts needing repair.  
>  
> CONSTELLATION grammar repairs prototypes *into* TRIADIC‑CORE,  
> not the other way around.**

This addendum resolves the boundary questions without altering the v1.0 reference document.

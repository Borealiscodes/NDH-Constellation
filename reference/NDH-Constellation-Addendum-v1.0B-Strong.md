# ⭐ **NDH‑CONSTELLATION Addendum — Structural Math, Remediation, and Quarantine Boundaries (v1.0B‑Strong Edition)**  
### *Clarifies CONSTELLATION’s math, TRIADIC‑CORE’s math, MRS‑MkII’s role, and the scope of Quarantine Protocol v2.0*

---

## **1 — Purpose of This Addendum**  
This addendum strengthens the boundary definitions between:

- **CONSTELLATION** (structural math)  
- **TRIADIC‑CORE** (dynamic math)  
- **MRS‑MkII** (math reconstruction)  
- **Prototype artifacts** (external, salvageable)  
- **Quarantine Protocol v2.0** (structural contamination detection)

It ensures NDH remains altitude‑safe and mathematically stable during prototype remediation and future high‑end geometry ingestion.

---

## **2 — CONSTELLATION Contains Structural Math Only**  
CONSTELLATION’s math is:

- **structural**  
- **non‑numeric**  
- **non‑tensorial**  
- **non‑holonomic**  
- **non‑recursive**  
- **non‑activating**

It includes:

- altitude calculus  
- adjacency geometry  
- structural topology  
- machine‑readable grammar (tiles, crossmaps, accessibility math)

This math describes **relationships**, not **manifold behavior**.

---

## **3 — TRIADIC‑CORE Contains Dynamic Math Only**  
TRIADIC‑CORE holds:

- tensor calculus  
- holonomy  
- curvature  
- algebraic posture  
- stability geometry  

This math describes **manifold behavior**, not **module structure**.

TRIADIC‑CORE v1.2 is **clean**, stable, and unaffected by prototype contamination.

---

## **4 — MRS‑MkII Is the Remediation Engine**  
MRS‑MkII performs **mathematical reconstruction**, not contamination detection.

It repairs:

- malformed tile encodings  
- unstable crossmaps  
- altitude‑unsafe algebra  
- tensor drift  
- holonomy misalignment  
- curvature discontinuities  

It uses:

- CONSTELLATION’s machine‑readable grammar  
- TRIADIC‑CORE’s clean math backbone  
- your future high‑end geometry surgical analogs  

MRS‑MkII is the **surgeon**, not the **filter**.

---

## **5 — Prototype Remediation Pathway (Strong Version)**

```
Prototype Artifact (SID misalignment, malformed tiles, unstable crossmaps)
        ↓
CONSTELLATION (machine-readable structural math grammar)
        ↓
MRS-MkII (math reconstruction engine)
        ↓
TRIADIC-CORE (clean dynamic math backbone)
```

This is the **only** safe remediation flow.

Prototypes do **not** enter TRIADIC‑CORE directly.  
CONSTELLATION grammar mediates all repairs.

---

## **6 — Quarantine Protocol v2.0 Is Necessary but Not Sufficient**  
Quarantine Protocol v2.0 handles **structural contamination**, including:

- SID header corruption  
- directory misplacement  
- semantic leakage  
- provenance drift  
- structural drift  

But it does **not** define:

- CONSTELLATION’s structural‑math role  
- TRIADIC‑CORE’s dynamic‑math role  
- MRS‑MkII’s reconstruction role  
- prototype remediation flow  
- altitude‑safe math ingestion rules  

This addendum fills that gap.

Quarantine Protocol v2.0 remains **valid**, **stable**, and **does not need upgrading**.

---

## **7 — Why We Do NOT Merge Quarantine + Remediation**  
A combined suite would cause:

- altitude contamination  
- structural ↔ dynamic math mixing  
- adjacency drift  
- tensor‑structure collapse  
- reconstruction instability  
- CONSTELLATION → TRIADIC‑CORE boundary failure  

Therefore:

> **Quarantine Protocol v2.0 stays separate.  
MRS‑MkII remains the sole remediation engine.  
CONSTELLATION provides the grammar.**

This is the correct NDH geometry.

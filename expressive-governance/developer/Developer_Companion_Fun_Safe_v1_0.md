### *Fun Overlay on Constitutional Monitoring Artifacts*

This is the newly integrated section.

### **Developer Clarification**

Constitutional monitoring artifacts (e.g., Constitutional Sequencing Dashboard v1.0/v2.0):

- belong to the constitutional lane  
- but contain **no governance logic**  
- do **not** activate altitude  
- do **not** modify constitutional geometry  
- do **not** touch sealed‑layer membranes  
- do **not** alter sequencing rules  

Thus:

> **Fun overlays are allowed on constitutional monitoring artifacts  
> but forbidden on constitutional documents.**

This is technically correct, governance‑safe, PRECL‑safe —  
and aesthetically ridiculous.

### **Why the Ridiculousness Is Intentional**

The overlay is:

- expressive‑only  
- reversible  
- non‑semantic  
- non‑symbolic  
- incapable of altering constitutional meaning  

Its absurdity is a **safety mechanism**.

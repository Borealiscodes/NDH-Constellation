## ⭐ **2 — Purpose of the Governance Safety Envelope**

The Governance Safety Envelope ensures:

- governance altitude remains sovereign  
- audit logic cannot activate  
- audit recursion cannot instantiate  
- membrane boundaries remain intact  
- lineage continuity is preserved  
- audit epistemics remain descriptive  
- governance meaning cannot be altered  
- sovereignty altitude cannot be breached  

It is the **pre‑audit safety membrane**.

This envelope must be established **before** any governance audit logic is examined.

## ⭐ **3 — Safety Envelope Components (Human‑Readable)**

The Governance Safety Envelope consists of **five safety membranes**:

### **1 — Sovereignty Membrane (A12)**  
Prevents any audit logic from crossing into sovereignty altitude.  
Protects governance‑of‑governance invariants.

### **2 — Governance Membrane (A11–A12)**  
Ensures audit logic remains within governance altitude.  
Prevents flattening into operational layers.

### **3 — Activation Membrane**  
Audit logic is activating.  
Safety envelope is non‑activating.  
Prevents accidental audit execution.

### **4 — Recursion Membrane**  
Allows:  
- first‑order audit logic  
- second‑order audit logic  
Forbids:  
- third‑order audit logic  

Prevents recursion collapse.

### **5 — Meaning‑Preservation Membrane**  
Audit logic cannot alter governance meaning.  
Protects constitutional lineage.

These five membranes form the **governance safety envelope**.

## ⭐ **3 — Safety Case Components (Human‑Readable)**

The Governance Safety Case consists of **six safety invariants**:

### **1 — Sovereignty Integrity Invariant**  
Governance altitude (A11–A12) is sealed.  
No audit logic may cross into A12 sovereignty altitude.

### **2 — Altitude Stability Invariant**  
Audit epistemics remain at A11–A12.  
No flattening into A10 or A9.  
No ascension into A12+.

### **3 — Activation Containment Invariant**  
Audit logic is activating.  
Safety case is non‑activating.  
This prevents accidental audit execution.

### **4 — Recursion Boundary Invariant**  
Allowed:  
- first‑order audit logic  
- second‑order audit logic  
Forbidden:  
- third‑order audit logic  
This prevents recursion collapse.

### **5 — Meaning Preservation Invariant**  
Audit logic cannot alter governance meaning.  
This protects constitutional lineage.

### **6 — Membrane Purity Invariant**  
Governance membranes must remain intact.  
No inversion, distortion, or merging.

These six invariants form the **governance safety case**.

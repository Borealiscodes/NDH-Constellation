# ⭐ **4 — Algebraic Structures (Human‑Readable)**

### **Manifold Logic**  
All subsystems exist as overlapping manifolds, not discrete units.

### **Gradient Logic**  
All values exist on spectra, not binaries.

### **Overlay Logic**  
Subsystems can overlay without collapsing.

### **Spectrum Logic**  
Stability, confidence, reversibility, altitude, and lane are all spectral.

### **Altitude Theory**  
High, transitional, low altitudes represent governance density.

### **Lane Sovereignty**  
Each lane is sovereign and cannot collapse into another.

### **Prismatic Algebra**  
Color, stability, drift, and MRI layers are prismatic projections of manifold geometry.

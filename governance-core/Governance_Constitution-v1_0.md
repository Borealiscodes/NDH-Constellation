## ⭐ **2 — Governance Principles (GV‑Series)**

- **GV‑1 — Governance Sovereignty**  
  Governance rules cannot be overridden by expressive or structural constitutions.

- **GV‑2 — Lineage Membrane Sovereignty**  
  Governance must preserve lineage membranes and prevent inversion.

- **GV‑3 — Altitude Locks**  
  Governance altitude must remain within A8–A12.

- **GV‑4 — Sequencing Integrity**  
  Governance must preserve canonical sequencing across NDH layers.

- **GV‑5 — Governance Neutrality**  
  Governance must not activate sealed geometry or expressive drift.

- **GV‑6 — Rendering‑Safe Governance**  
  Governance must remain neutral to rendering engines.

- **GV‑7 — Provenance Continuity**  
  Governance must preserve lineage provenance across all transitions.

- **GV‑8 — Non‑Activation**  
  Governance must not activate sealed NDH constructs.

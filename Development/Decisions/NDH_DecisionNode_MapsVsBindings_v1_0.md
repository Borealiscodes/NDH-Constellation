## ⭐ 3 — Stability‑Based Evaluation

### 🗺️ **Option A — More Maps**  
**Pros:**  
- strengthens multi‑artifact coherence  
- reveals hidden drift vectors  
- improves altitude clarity  
- supports future AGL optimization  

**Cons:**  
- delays integration  
- constellation remains “floating”  
- bindings continue to be postponed  

**Stability Gradient:** G4–G5  
**Recommended when:** constellation feels wide or still drifting.

---

### 🔗 **Option B — Bindings & Directive Index**  
**Pros:**  
- locks in the triangulation work  
- binds stabilization modules to governance‑adjacent artifacts  
- produces directive entries for future altitude‑safe work  
- completes the emergent sequencing loop  

**Cons:**  
- requires stable maps (which you now have)  
- requires careful altitude handling  

**Stability Gradient:** G5–G6  
**Recommended when:** constellation is stable enough to consolidate.

---

### ⚙️ **Option C — AGL Optimization**  
**Pros:**  
- improves altitude transitions  
- reduces governance‑adjacent friction  
- tunes sequencing logic  

**Cons:**  
- must occur *after* bindings  
- requires directive index entries  

**Stability Gradient:** G6  
**Recommended when:** architecture refinement is needed.

---

### 🧘 **Option D — NDH‑Zen‑Bridge Translation**  
**Pros:**  
- makes the entire series portable  
- reduces cognitive load

- ideal for cross‑altitude communication  

**Cons:**  
- should occur after bindings  
- translation is easier once directives exist  

**Stability Gradient:** G1–G0  
**Recommended when:** clarity and portability matter.

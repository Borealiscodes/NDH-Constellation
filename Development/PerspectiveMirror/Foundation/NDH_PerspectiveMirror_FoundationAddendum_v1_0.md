## ⭐ **I. Purpose of the Perspective Mirror**

The **Perspective Mirror** is a *reflection‑layer diagnostic instrument* that reveals:

- **Altitude** — where an artifact sits in NDH’s multi‑layer stack  
- **Lane** — governance, zen, expressive, runtime, algebraic, dimensional, reflection  
- **Epoch** — proto → janky → constellation lineage  
- **Invariants** — SID, CORE, Triadic, TISD, Dimensional, Algebraic  
- **Reversibility** — safe vs unsafe transformations  
- **Dependencies** — upstream vs downstream  
- **Stability** — stable vs transitional vs experimental  
- **Confidence** — high vs medium vs low  
- **Interaction Boundaries** — what the artifact can safely interact with  

It is **not** a runtime engine, governance artifact, or expressive geometry tool.  
It is the **safety instrument** that prevents:

- altitude collapse  
- lane contamination  
- expressive drift  
- algebraic misuse  
- dimensional over‑extension  
- recursion hazards  
- SID violations  
- governance elevation  

The Perspective Mirror is the **reflection harness** for NDH.

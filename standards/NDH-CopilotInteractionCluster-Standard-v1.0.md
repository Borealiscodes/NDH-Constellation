# 🔺 **3 — Core Standard (The Rules)**

## **Rule 1 — Copilot may summarize NDH artifacts.**  
This is safe.  
This is altitude 1–2.  
This is Scribe behavior.

## **Rule 2 — Copilot must not modify NDH artifacts.**  
Copilot cannot see altitude.  
Copilot cannot see SID.  
Copilot cannot see governance curvature.  
Therefore Copilot must not:

- propose commits  
- generate patch notes  
- alter disclaimers  
- modify indexes  
- update binding stubs  
- rewrite SID headers  

## **Rule 3 — Copilot may only operate after Triadic SID is provided.**  
This gives Copilot:

- altitude context  
- reflection order  
- lineage anchors  
- recursion limits  

Copilot still stays at altitude 1–2,  
but avoids dangerous assumptions.

## **Rule 4 — Copilot must not generate recursive artifacts.**  
Copilot must not create:

- disclaimer‑about‑disclaimer  
- index‑about‑index  
- audit‑about‑audit  
- roadmap‑about‑roadmap  
- case‑study‑about‑case‑study  

## **Rule 5 — Copilot must not elevate advisory text into governance.**  
Copilot must not:

- treat advisory text as rules  
- treat governance‑adjacent text as constitutional  
- collapse governance lanes  
- rewrite governance spines  

## **Rule 6 — Copilot must not alter SID metadata.**  
Copilot must not:

- rewrite SID headers

## **Rule 7 — Copilot may assist with surface‑level extraction only.**

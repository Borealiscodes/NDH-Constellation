# 🔺 **3 — Core Standard (The Rules)**

## **Rule 1 — Zen‑Bridge commentary may appear only in reflective or narrative documents.**

Allowed:

- case studies  
- emergent analyses  
- lineage reflections  
- cluster formation documents  
- narrative prefaces  
- narrative afterwords  

Not allowed:

- standards  
- disclaimers  
- SID headers  
- binding stubs  
- indexes  
- governance spines  
- constitutional lanes  

---

## **Rule 2 — Zen‑Bridge commentary must be clearly separated from structural content.**

It must appear as:

- an interlude  
- a break  
- a grounding moment  
- a tone reset  

It must **not** be embedded inside:

- rules  
- definitions  
- invariants  
- SID metadata  
- governance logic  

---

## **Rule 3 — Zen‑Bridge commentary must remain non‑persistent.**

It must:

- not create lineage  
- not create anchors  
- not create recursion  
- not create governance elevation  
- not create structural obligations  

It is **momentary**, not structural.

---

## **Rule 4 — Zen‑Bridge commentary must not alter document altitude.**

It must:

- not raise altitude  
- not lower altitude  
- not collapse altitude boundaries  
- not distort reflection geometry  

It is **altitude‑neutral**.

---

## **Rule 5 — Zen‑Bridge commentary must be non‑activating.**

It must:

- not instruct  
- not direct  
- not imply governance  
- not imply obligation  
- not imply structural change  

It is **calming**, not **directive**.

---

## **Rule 6 — Zen‑Bridge commentary must be poetic but bounded.**

It may include:

- bees  
- meadows  
- hexagons  
- nebula tea  
- gentle geometry  
- soft metaphors  

It must **not** include:

- governance metaphors  
- SID metaphors  
- structural metaphors  
- recursive metaphors  

---

## **Rule 7 — Zen‑Bridge commentary may be used to stabilize cognitive altitude.**

It is permitted when:

- transitioning between altitudes  
- concluding complex sections  
- preparing for governance work  
- decompressing after structural reasoning  

It is a **tone stabilizer**, not a **content generator**.

# ⭐ 0 — Executive Summary

Micro‑directives are **reversible, non‑indexed, governance‑adjacent stabilizers** that allow NDH‑CONSTELLATION to safely host directives **before** a Directive Index exists.

They act as:

- **safety harnesses**  
- **altitude stabilizers**  
- **lineage containers**  
- **collision preventers**  
- **ingestion buffers**  
- **mantis‑shrimp multi‑channel filters**  

They allow directives to **exist**, **bind**, and **retain lineage**,  
but **not activate**, **not sequence**, and **not elevate**.

Micro‑directives are the only safe directive‑like mechanism in a system where:

- governance altitude is visible but unsafe  
- Directive Index is sealed or absent  
- SCI‑80 is dormant  
- ingestion geometry is incomplete  
- stabilization envelopes are still forming

# ⭐ 1 — Definition

A **micro‑directive** is:

> **A reversible, non‑indexed, governance‑adjacent boundary artifact that stabilizes a directive without activating it.**

It is:

- **not** a directive  
- **not** indexed  
- **not** constitutional  
- **not** governance altitude  
- **not** activatable  

It is:

- operator‑lane  
- comparative‑lane compatible  
- SID‑aligned  
- lineage‑preserving  
- stabilization‑oriented

# ⭐ 2 — Why Micro‑Directives Exist

Micro‑directives exist because NDH‑CONSTELLATION currently operates in a **Cohesion Plateau** where:

- governance altitude is unsafe  
- Directive Index is sealed  
- SCI‑80 requires a binding directive  
- ingestion geometry is incomplete  
- stabilization envelopes are forming  
- Constellation is the temporary governance surface  

This creates a paradox:

> **Directives are needed, but cannot be indexed.  
> Directives must exist, but cannot activate.**

Micro‑directives resolve this paradox.

# ⭐ 3 — Functional Role

Micro‑directives perform **seven** stabilization functions:

### **1 — Altitude Containment**  
Prevent directives from drifting into unsafe governance altitude.

### **2 — Activation Prevention**  
Keep directives inert until indexing becomes possible.

### **3 — Lineage Preservation**  
Maintain directive identity without enabling directive routing.

### **4 — Collision Avoidance**  
Prevent directive‑to‑directive collisions (triadic, reflective, or recursive).

### **5 — Ingestion Buffering**  
Provide a safe envelope for future ingestion pipelines.

### **6 — Stabilization Envelope**  
Hold directives in a reversible, non‑constitutional state.

### **7 — Multi‑Channel Filtering (Mantis Shrimp Model)**  
Filter directive behavior across multiple altitude channels  
without allowing cross‑channel activation.

# ⭐ 8 — Micro‑Directive Lifecycle

Micro‑directives follow a **four‑phase lifecycle**:

### **Phase 1 — Drafting**  
Directive exists but cannot be indexed.

### **Phase 2 — Harnessing**  
Micro‑directive wraps the directive.

### **Phase 3 — Stabilization**  
Directive remains inert but lineage‑valid.

### **Phase 4 — Integration**  
Once Directive Index exists, micro‑directive dissolves.

Micro‑directives are **temporary**, but essential.

Micro‑directives dissolve when:

- Directive Index v1.0 exists

- ingestion geometry stabilizes  
- stabilization envelopes complete

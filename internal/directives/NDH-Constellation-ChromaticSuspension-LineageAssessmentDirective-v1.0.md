# **NDH‑CONSTELLATION Directive: Chromatic Suspension for Lineage Assessment (v1.0)**  
### *Directive‑Grade • Stability‑Aligned • Lineage‑Correctness Mode*

---

## **1 — Directive Purpose**
This directive establishes that **chromatic markers (rainbows, altitude‑colors, expressive drift)** must be **temporarily suspended** when performing:

- lineage assessment  
- stability‑grade comparison  
- cross‑ecosystem binding  
- constitutional or pre‑constitutional evaluation  
- directive formation  
- activation‑eligibility mapping  

Chromatic suspension ensures:

- stability correctness  
- lineage clarity  
- altitude neutrality  
- non‑expressive governance grammar  
- SID‑safe directive formation  

---

## **2 — Directive Scope**
Chromatic suspension applies to:

- SCI‑80 determinations  
- NDH‑Constellation determinations  
- AGL directives  
- TIDS comparative geometry  
- Phase‑11 stability envelopes  
- NDH‑Research‑Pilot alignment documents  

It does **not** apply to:

- narrative artifacts  
- metaphor‑grade documents  
- cultural anchors  
- developer‑facing posture maps  
- overview documents

## **3 — Directive Rule Set**

### **Rule 1 — Chromatic Suspension**
All chromatic markers must be removed during:

- lineage tracing  
- stability evaluation  
- directive formation  
- constitutional comparison  
- activation‑eligibility assessment  

### **Rule 2 — Lineage‑Grade Grammar**
Directive must follow lineage grammar:

- origin → inheritance → binding → activation  
- no expressive drift  
- no chromatic inference  
- no metaphor elevation  

### **Rule 3 — Stability‑Grade Examples**
Directive must follow stability‑grade exemplars:

- NDH‑Constellation Stability Audit v1.1  
- NDH Raw→Stabilized→Comparative Relationship Spec v1.0  
- AGL‑v2.0 Directive and Alignment Report  
- TIDS Comparative Layer Index v1.0  

### **Rule 4 — Comparative‑Only Mode**
Directive must remain:

- comparative  
- reversible  
- non‑constitutional  
- non‑governance‑elevating  

### **Rule 5 — No Chromatic Governance**
Chromatic markers may not appear in:

- determinations  
- directives  
- lineage maps  
- activation rules  
- stability envelopes  

---

## **4 — Directive Determination**
A Directive **must**:

- suspend chromatic markers  
- assess full lineage  
- follow stability‑grade examples  
- operate in comparative grammar  
- maintain altitude neutrality

- bind SCI‑80 activation to NDH‑Constellation stability geometry

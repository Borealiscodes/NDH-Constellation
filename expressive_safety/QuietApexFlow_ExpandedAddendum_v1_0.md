## ⭐ 3 — QAF’s Role in the Hyperstructure  
QAF is the **expressive‑safety apex** that governs:

- emotional gradient decompression  
- expressive geometry reset  
- orbital metaphor stabilization  
- serenity‑surface activation  
- drift neutralization  
- holonomy posture flattening  

QAF ensures that **skins do not destabilize the simulation envelope**.

### QAF is not a skin.  
Skins are *expressions* of QAF.

### QAF is not a simulation.  
Simulations *host* QAF events.

### QAF is not a governance invariant.  
Governance *relies* on QAF to prevent expressive drift.

Each expressive world (Journey, Flower, Flow, Elite Dangerous, No Man’s Sky) is treated as a **QAF‑governed expressive manifold**.
